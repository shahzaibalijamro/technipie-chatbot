## Company Overview

Technipie is a software development and digital solutions agency that helps startups, small businesses, and established companies build, improve, and maintain digital products.

Technipie provides services in web development, mobile app development, UI/UX design, WordPress development, artificial intelligence, custom software development, e-commerce development, API development, and ongoing technical support.

Technipie works with clients from multiple industries, including e-commerce, healthcare, education, real estate, finance, logistics, restaurants, professional services, and technology startups.

The agency focuses on building practical digital products that are scalable, maintainable, and easy for clients to operate.

Technipie can work with clients from the initial idea and requirements stage through design, development, testing, deployment, and post-launch maintenance.

Technipie offers both fixed-price projects and dedicated development engagements depending on the requirements of the client.

# SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

Technipie's typical development process consists of several stages.

## Stage 1: Discovery

The team meets with the client to understand the business, target users, requirements, goals, and technical constraints.

The team identifies the major features and creates an initial project scope.

## Stage 2: Planning

Technipie creates a development plan based on the requirements.

The team determines the technology stack, architecture, milestones, and estimated timeline.

## Stage 3: UI/UX Design

Designers create wireframes and high-fidelity interfaces.

The client reviews the designs before development begins.

## Stage 4: Development

Developers implement the frontend, backend, database, integrations, and other required functionality.

Development is usually divided into smaller milestones.

## Stage 5: Testing

The application is tested for functional problems, usability issues, performance problems, and security concerns.

Issues discovered during testing are fixed before production deployment.

## Stage 6: Deployment

The completed application is deployed to the client's production environment.

Technipie can configure domains, SSL certificates, databases, servers, and other infrastructure.

## Stage 7: Launch and Support

After launch, Technipie can continue providing maintenance, bug fixes, monitoring, and additional feature development.

# DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

Technipie prefers an iterative development approach.

Large projects are divided into smaller milestones so that clients can review progress throughout development.

Clients can provide feedback during development rather than waiting until the entire project is finished.

For complex projects, Technipie may recommend developing a Minimum Viable Product first.

An MVP contains the minimum set of features required to validate the product idea with real users.

After validating the MVP, additional features can be developed in later phases.

Project timelines depend on project complexity.

A simple business website may take between 1 and 3 weeks.

A custom website with multiple pages and integrations may take between 3 and 6 weeks.

A medium-sized web application may take between 6 and 12 weeks.

A complex SaaS platform or custom software product can require several months.

Mobile applications typically require between 8 and 16 weeks depending on complexity.

AI projects can take between 3 and 12 weeks depending on the AI functionality, integrations, and amount of custom development required.

These estimates are general guidelines and do not represent guaranteed delivery dates.
